A small property, booked one family at a time

Hill View is built around bamboo — the cottages, the shade over the deck, the screens around the pool. Nothing here is big. Three cottages sit along the slope, each with its own verandah facing the valley, and the lawn below holds the fire and the evening.

Take one cottage for a quiet two days, or take the whole property and bring everyone.

  • Private verandah and valley view with every cottage
  • Pool, bonfire pit and open lawn shared by the property
  • Home-style meals cooked on order — veg and non-veg
  • Parking inside the gate, drive right up to your door
